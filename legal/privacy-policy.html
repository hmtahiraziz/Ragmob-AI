Data we collect

  • Account data: email, name, password (hashed by Clerk), optional profile photo

  • Device permissions you grant (e.g. camera, photos, or microphone when you use those features)

How we use data

  • Authenticate you and sync conversations
  • Generate AI responses via our API and model provider
  • Review reports for safety and abuse

Sharing

We share account data with Clerk and chat content with our backend and LLM provider as needed to operate the service. We do not sell personal information or use ads/ad IDs.

Retention & deletion

You can delete chats or your entire account in Settings. You can also request deletion via the account deletion page. Residual backups may be removed within 30 days.
