Delete your Ragmob account

Google Play and privacy laws require a clear way to delete your account and associated data. Use either method below.

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Ragmob and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm twice.

This deletes your Clerk login, server-side chats, feedback, reports, and local chat history on the device.

Email fallback

If you cannot open the app, email privacy@ragmob.app from the address on your account. Include “Account deletion request” in the subject. We will delete your account and related data within 30 days.

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
